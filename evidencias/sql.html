Ejemplo de consulta — empleados por departamento

Consulta que relaciona EMPLOYEES y DEPARTMENTS, agrupa los empleados por departamento y aplica condiciones sobre los resultados agregados.

SELECT
    d.department_id,
    d.department_name,
    COUNT(e.employee_id) AS total_empleados,
    AVG(e.salary) AS salario_promedio
FROM hr.departments d
JOIN hr.employees e
    ON e.department_id = d.department_id
GROUP BY
    d.department_id,
    d.department_name
HAVING COUNT(e.employee_id) > 2
   AND AVG(e.salary) > 5000
ORDER BY AVG(e.salary) DESC;

Ejemplo de práctica con JOIN, GROUP BY, HAVING, COUNT, AVG y ORDER BY.
